## Scalar variation

For $\mathcal L=-\frac12g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\varphi\partial_\nu\varphi-U$,

$$\delta S=\int\sqrt{-g}[-\nabla^\mu\varphi\nabla_\mu\delta\varphi-U_{,\varphi}\delta\varphi]d^4x
=\int\sqrt{-g}(\Box\varphi-U_{,\varphi})\delta\varphi\,d^4x.$$

Boundary terms vanish for compactly supported variations. Thus $\Box\varphi-U_{,\varphi}=0$. A positive quadratic potential produces $\Box\varphi-m^2\varphi=0$ under (-+++). With $\varphi\propto e^{-i\omega t+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$, this gives $\omega^2=|\mathbf k|^2+m^2$. The displayed plus-mass source equation instead gives $\omega^2=|\mathbf k|^2-m^2$ if its box and mass have these same meanings. That is not the frozen positive-mass branch.

The source's alternative ME-002/ME-004 branch uses (+---) and an additional alpha-dependent time term. It is preserved as a distinct branch, not silently identified with C1 or the root scalar equation.

## Scalar interactions

$$\partial_a(g_{ab}\varphi_a\varphi_b)=g_{ab}\varphi_b,\qquad
\partial_a(h_{abc}\varphi_a\varphi_b\varphi_c)=h_{abc}\varphi_b\varphi_c.$$

The same coefficient appears in the reciprocal equation. With canonical kinetic terms, a source assigning unrelated constants to these two variations does not supply a common potential unless reciprocity is imposed. The audit records every directional source alias and the canonical coefficient to which it is mapped. Constants are symbolic; no best-fit coefficient is used to manufacture a pass.

## Gauge variation

At fixed metric and scalars,

$$\delta X=2F^{\mu\nu}\delta F_{\mu\nu}=4F^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\delta A_\nu.$$

Therefore

$$\delta_A S=-\int\sqrt{-g}(1+4U_X)F^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\delta A_\nu
=\int\sqrt{-g}\nabla_\mu[(1+4U_X)F^{\mu\nu}]\delta A_\nu.$$

The same argument applies to H. If a matter action is added, define $j_M^\nu=-(1/\sqrt{-g})\delta S_m/\delta A_\nu$, with an analogous S current. The catalogue comparison freezes these external currents to zero; unspecified Xi objects are not assumed to take the values needed to pass.

The divergence of the phenomenological neutral-scalar current $\varphi\nabla^\nu\varphi$ is

$$\nabla_\nu(\varphi\nabla^\nu\varphi)=(\nabla\varphi)^2+\varphi\Box\varphi.$$

For a free massless field $\varphi=t$ on Minkowski space this equals -1. Consequently the current is not generically conserved even on that scalar solution. It is not generated by the frozen gauge-invariant action of neutral real scalars. This does not rule out other charged-field models; those are additional theories.

The historical proposal's $-\gamma\varphi^2\nabla_\mu\varphi A^\mu$ term also has a different power of phi from the catalogue current. Under $A\mapsto A+\nabla\chi$ it changes by a term that is not generically a boundary term. Its scalar variation must be included if it is retained. C1 excludes it.

## Metric variation

Use

$$\delta\sqrt{-g}=-\tfrac12\sqrt{-g}g_{\mu\nu}\delta g^{\mu\nu},\quad
\delta X=2F_{\mu\rho}F_\nu{}^\rho\delta g^{\mu\nu},$$

and the analogous identity for Y. Integration by parts in the Ricci variation gives

$$\int\sqrt{-g}\,U_R\delta R=\int\sqrt{-g}[U_RR_{\mu\nu}+(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu)U_R]\delta g^{\mu\nu}.$$

Apply $T_{\mu\nu}=-2(\sqrt{-g})^{-1}\delta S_{nonEH}/\delta g^{\mu\nu}$ to obtain the full T(U) in CANDIDATE.md. In particular, for $U=\xi R\varphi^2/2$,

$$T^{(U)}_{\mu\nu}=\xi[\varphi^2G_{\mu\nu}+(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu)\varphi^2].$$

The original proposal's description of scalar stresses as merely canonical omits this contribution unless it is explicitly included elsewhere. C1 includes it.

## Conservation and external sources

For this closed, diffeomorphism-invariant action, the appropriate total stress obeys the covariant conservation identity on solutions of all field equations. Individual interacting sectors need not be separately conserved. Prescribed nondynamical sources can exchange energy/momentum with the fields.

The earlier master note said stress conservation holds only for its gauge-invariant truncation. Diffeomorphism invariance and gauge invariance are distinct conditions: breaking the latter does not, by itself, remove the former Noether identity when all dynamical fields are varied. The excluded gamma sector still has its own consistency obligations.
